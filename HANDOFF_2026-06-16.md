## 0. The single most important finding: balance is gated on the CPU, not the numbers
The weapon-balance simulator (`harness/tests/bbbalance.js`, seeded CPU-vs-CPU, every matchup → win-rate matrix) revealed that **front-armor immunity + a CPU that turtles front-to-front = only front-piercing weapons ever connect.** Before the flanking brain: 1% of matches were decisive, everything drew, and tuning damage numbers did NOTHING (winners never changed). After **P0 = the flanking brain** (`bbCpuUpdate` now ORBITS to the foe's exposed rear/side while keeping its own front+weapon pointed at the foe — movement is holonomic in RoboRumble so it can strafe): 68% decisive, and weapons spread out (piston 4%→75%, ram 0%→50%).
**Takeaway for whoever continues:** always re-run `bbbalance.js` after a weapon change. Numbers are meaningless without the AI being able to use the weapon. Current balance: **spinner 75% · piston 75% · ram/none 50% · flame 25% · wedge 0%.** Flame + wedge lag because they still lack their P1 kit (below) — that's the next work, NOT more number-tweaking.

## 1. WEAPONS — the full roster vision (Sam's combat-robot model)
The internal weapon id stays `'wedge'` etc. (don't churn ids); change DISPLAY names + behavior. Armor types: BALANCED / HARDPLATE / HEATSHIELD / LIGHT (the `BB_RPS` weapon-vs-armor triangle exists).

### SPINNER (kinetic) — DONE (still tunable)
Sam's model, all implemented:
- **Takes a while to spin up** (`spinRamp`); **the longer it spins, the more damage** (`spinDmg × spin`).
- **Loses inertia/RPM every time it hits something** — "maybe you have to restart every time you hit something, or it just loses momentum." Implemented as `spinHitLoss` ≈ near-restart, so it must re-spin between bites.
- **Self-damages on every bite** (energy transfer, `spinSelf`) — "spinner should also damage the spinner bot."
- **Self-damages + bleeds spin on hitting a WALL** (`spinWallDmg`/`spinWallLoss`).
- **A bulldozer blade / passive defensive weapon DEFLECTS it** (`spinDeflect`): the disc fully stalls + shreds itself — this is the **anti-spinner counter** ("self damage … when hitting a bulldozer blade or something a passive defensive weapon"). NOTE: the deflect self-damages the spinner, but the wedge currently has no offense to capitalize → wedge still loses the matchup until its grab-slam (P1) lands.
- **Front-pierce** (`spinFront`): a spinner bites whatever it touches, armor facing be damned.
- Was **"a little too overpowered"** → fixed by **more HP for everyone** (not a pure nerf) + the RPM-loss/self-damage mechanics.

### PISTON / HAMMER (armor-piercer) — DONE (air-tank DEFERRED)
- "Hammer bots try to pierce the robot's armor" → **front-pierce** (`pistFront`): the strike cracks the armored front (RAM contact stays front-immune). This is its identity: the anti-turtle weapon.
- "Real piston bots fling robots up in the air and they crash down, sometimes outside the arena" — **top-down can't fling up**, so that fantasy is split off into the **FLIPPER** (below); the piston is the armor-piercer.
- **Feedback fix (DONE):** the hammer now ALWAYS visibly extends + thumps on a fire press (was a silent dry-swing on a miss → felt dead); fire = **RT or A** on a gamepad (A added in case a pad reports RT only as an axis).
- **DEFERRED — air-pressure reservoir:** limited strikes that refill (like the flame fuel idea). Sam: "if flamethrower and piston were more competitive I'd add the air and fuel to balance them. oh you could even have a refuel spot [on the map]." So: only add the air-tank + refuel pad once piston is strong enough to need the constraint.

### FLAMETHROWER (thermal) — range/cone DONE, FUEL DEFERRED, TURRET-AIM = next P1
- More **range** + **wider cone** (DONE). Has a blow-up meter (sustained burn detonates).
- **FUEL gauge: built then REMOVED** — Sam decided "the fuel, and air idea is too complicated" for now. It would come back (with a map **refuel spot**) only if flame/piston need balancing.
- **TURRET-AIM (NOT yet built — top of P1):** "is your flamethrower, or any TURRETED weapon, can be aimed separately with right stick or mouse, REGARDLESS of mouse-aiming on or off … it isn't just locked forward." Mirror the tank-cannon aim (`tankTurretAim`/`tankAimCannon`, v5.1.74): give the flame a `weaponAng` aimed by the gamepad right-stick / mouse (independent of the [K] Mouse-Aim toggle), fire the cone along it, and draw the cone at `weaponAng`. This is what lets flame track flankers and fixes its ~25% sim rate.

### BULLDOZER BLADE (rename from WEDGE) — render + grab-slam = next P1
- "We should probably have NO RAM ONLY, I think that is covered by the wedge." → **drop the RAM-ONLY (`none`) pickable option**; the blade is the baseline pusher. (Keep `'none'` internally as the neutral default if cheaper, just don't offer it in the armory.)
- "The wedge should be rendered/drawn like the front of a bulldozer … real-life wedge battlebots don't look like that. Maybe it should be called something else for a bulldozer blade." → **render a real bulldozer BLADE** (a low front plow, not the current triangle) and **rename the display** (suggested **"DOZER"** or "BLADE" — Sam's call; keep id `'wedge'`).
- "Those [blades] are good for defense against spinners." → already the **spinner DEFLECT** counter (above).
- **Soft sticky GRAB-AND-SLAM (the key un-built mechanic):** "maybe the bulldozer blade gets a soft sticky action where if you ram them you hold on for a second and can slam them into a wall." → on a firing front-arc contact, GRAB the foe (hold ~0.6–1s, foe stuck to the blade's front), drive it around, and if you SLAM it into a wall it takes damage. This is what gives the blade OFFENSE and fixes its 0% sim rate. Synergizes with TANK drive (pushing).

### FLIPPER (new) — un-built
- "I would like a robot that flings the other robot BACK, because we can't fling up in the air in a top-down game." → a big horizontal knockback launcher; on a firing front-arc hit it flings the foe away. Sets up **ring-outs** into arena hazards (see §4).

### PINCER (new) — un-built
- "I want a bot that grabs and immobilizes the other robot, like a pincer bot or claw, but I like pincer bot." "Only focus on pushing and pinning the other robot." "This wouldn't be a weapon." → a control TYPE: grabs + immobilizes (hold/pin), no real damage.
- "Not as good for 1v1, but in a 3v3 could be a role player." → design it as a **3v3 role-player**: hold a foe while teammates pile on; weak solo.

## 8. COUNT-OUT + DASH rules (DONE — but capturing the intent so it's not "fixed back")
- **Count-out is STALEMATE-ONLY.** "I don't want a mobility-knockout countdown UNLESS nothing is happening — both bots are not moving … because I want to give players the satisfaction of finishing off an immobile opponent." So: an immobilized bot taps out ONLY if its side has no mobile foe left. If a foe can still move, let the player earn the kill. (HUD shows "⚠ N" only during a real count-out, "⚠ STUCK" otherwise.) **Do not regress this to a per-bot count-out.**
- **RAM/DASH = forward lunge along the NOSE,** not the stick direction. "I want ram/dash to only work in the forward direction of the bot." You ram with your armored front.

## 12. WORKFLOW + gotchas (so a fresh thread can ship in minutes)
- **Single self-contained HTML file**; all code in one inline `<script>`; changelog comment block near the end; no external assets/deps.
- **Release ritual (per version):** edit `drive_showdown_vX.Y.Z.html` → `git mv` to the next version → update `extract.sh`'s hardcoded filename → bump the header `file:`/`version:` lines → prepend a changelog entry → update the harness suites → `./extract.sh` (writes `/tmp/g.js`, runs `node --check`) → `./battery.sh` (must print ALL GREEN + ALL SCENARIOS BALANCED) → commit `Release vX.Y.Z: …`.
- **Tests:** `harness/tests/smoke*.js` (each evals the game source + an appended IIFE that asserts; they read `/tmp/g.js`). `battery.sh` runs them all + `balance.js`. RoboRumble tests live in **smoke57**. **`bbbalance.js` is a standalone REPORT** (not in the battery) — run it by hand to tune weapons.
- **Harness gotcha:** `getInp` must run inside `withBot(...)` (it reads the global `robot`); the `'cpu'`/`'remote'` branches return early so they're safe bare, the kb path is NOT.
- **Movement in RoboRumble is HOLONOMIC** (`b.x += inp.vx*dt` directly in world space) regardless of the drive type — the drive type only affects the visual + wheel-fault family. That's why the flanking brain can strafe and why turret-aim is needed (the chassis facing ≠ where it must move).
- **Key RoboRumble landmarks:** `BB`/`BB_W` (all tunable combat constants), `bbApplyHit(victim,loc,dmg,owner,fromX,fromY,frontPierce)`, `bbCpuUpdate` (flanking brain + per-weapon `brain.fire`), `bbWeaponPre`/`bbWeaponFire` (spin/cooldown/flame), `updateBB` (movement + contact loop + count-out), `drawBB`/`bbDrawWeapon` (render), the ARMORY (`bbArmory*`, `bbArmEquip`), the grid (`drawTankGrid`/`tankGridClick`).
